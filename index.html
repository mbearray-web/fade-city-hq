Before you book.

How do I book with Claudio?

Select “Book your appointment” to open Claudio’s Booksy page. Choose your service and available time, then complete the booking there.

Do you offer kids’ haircuts?

Yes. Kids’ haircuts and fades for ages 12 and under are listed at $30 for a 30-minute appointment.

Does the haircut and beard service include a hot towel?

Yes. The haircut and beard service includes a hot towel and razor finish. It is listed at $50 for 45 minutes.

What if I can’t find an available appointment?

Text Claudio at (832) 792-8902 to ask about availability. Before- and after-hours services are also listed on Booksy.
